Text messaging terms

Last updated: November 21, 2026

What the program is

Electro Service, Inc. runs a work board at electroservice.net where companies ("providers") book independent IT field technicians ("techs") for specific jobs. Users can choose to receive the chat messages about their jobs by text and reply by text. Texts are sent from Electro Service's business number, (859) 710-9917. Neither side sees the other's phone number.

How you opt in

Texting is off by default. A signed-in user turns it on in Settings by entering a US mobile number and ticking an unchecked box that reads: "Yes, text me job messages from Electro Service at this number. Message frequency varies with your jobs. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help." We then text a 6-digit code to the number, and texts start only after the user enters that code on the site.

What we send

Only messages about jobs you're involved in: new chat messages from the provider or tech on a job, and verification codes. No marketing or promotional texts. Message frequency varies with your jobs.

Example: "Electro Service: Acme IT re job #142: Badge in at the front desk. Reply to answer. Reply STOP to opt out."

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to any text to stop all texts. You can also untick the box in Settings. Reply START to turn texts back on. Reply HELP for help, or email info@electroservice.net.

Costs and carriers

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your phone number is used only to deliver these texts. We do not sell it or share it, or your opt-in, with third parties for marketing. Texts are relayed into the job's chat on electroservice.net and may be reviewed by Electro Service as described in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
