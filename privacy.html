Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, company, password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Tech profiles: skills, certifications, insurance documents and your address. Your address is used only to calculate distances and is never shown to other users.
  • Work orders, chat messages, texts sent through our number, and files you upload.
  • Phone numbers, if you opt in to text messages. Your number is never shown to the other party. See our texting terms.
  • Activity on the site, such as logins, IP addresses and actions taken.

How we use it

To run the work board, match techs and providers, process payments, prevent fraud, resolve disputes and keep the platform safe. Electro Service staff may review messages, texts and activity for these purposes.

Who we share it with

Stripe (payments, identity verification and tax forms), our text message provider (to deliver texts), and our hosting provider. We share profile details between a tech and a provider only as needed for a work order. We do not sell your information. We do not share text message opt-in data with third parties for marketing.

Your choices

You can update your profile, stop texts by replying STOP, or ask us to close your account by emailing info@electroservice.net. Some records are kept as required for payments, taxes and disputes.
