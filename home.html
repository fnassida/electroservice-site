What you'll learn

  • Setting up the business

    Choosing a structure, registering, insurance, a business bank account and the paperwork clients will ask for.

  • Pricing your work

    Hourly, flat-rate and contract pricing, what techs in your market charge, and how not to undercharge.

  • Finding work

    Field service marketplaces like WorkMarket and Field Nation, subcontracting for larger firms, and winning local clients of your own.

  • Doing the job like a business

    Work orders, scheduling, documenting jobs and handling a client who isn't happy.

  • Getting paid and keeping the books

    Invoicing, chasing late payments, bookkeeping, 1099s and setting money aside for taxes.

  • Growing past yourself

    Repeat clients, service contracts, bringing on other techs and knowing when you're ready to.

Who it's for

  • Field techs taking marketplace jobs who want clients of their own.
  • IT staff thinking about going independent and wondering what it takes.
  • Techs with a side business who are ready to make it their main income.

About Frank B. Nassida

Frank is a systems architect and technical consultant based in Kentucky, with more than 40 years in the computer industry and a degree in information technology from Strayer University, summa cum laude.

  • 1994–now

    Founder, Electro Service, Inc.

    Custom computer manufacturing in the thousands of units, nationwide deployment, and network architecture for multi-state contracts.

  • 1988–1994

    IBM Strategic Account Manager, Hamilton Avnet / Arrow Electronics

    Northeast liaison for IBM research facilities, including supercomputer sub-assembly contracts.

  • 1977–1982

    Franchise Group Manager, Tandy / Radio Shack

    Ran a 124-store franchise group across four states.
