Who we are

Electro Service, Inc. ("Electro Service", "we") runs electroservice.net, including the work board where providers post IT field service work orders and independent technicians ("techs") request and complete them.

Accounts

You must give accurate information and keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account. We may suspend or close accounts that break these terms.

Techs are independent

Techs are independent businesses, not employees or agents of Electro Service. Techs choose which work to accept and are responsible for their own work, tools, taxes, licenses and insurance.

Insurance

Techs must keep current liability insurance and upload proof of it. Techs without approved, current insurance cannot be offered work orders. Providers may set higher coverage requirements for their jobs.

Work orders, fees and payment

Work orders must be at least $50.00. Providers pay the job amount plus a 10% platform fee when a tech is booked. Payments are processed by Stripe. Electro Service holds the payment and releases the job amount to the tech after the provider approves the completed work. Refunds and disputes are handled under the work order's terms and Stripe's rules. Techs must complete Stripe's account verification to be paid.

Messages and texts

Techs and providers can chat on the site and, if they opt in, by text message through an Electro Service number. Message frequency varies with your jobs. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts at any time, or HELP for help. See our texting terms.

Monitoring

To keep the work board safe and resolve disputes, Electro Service may review any content on the platform, including messages, texts sent through our number, work orders, uploads and account activity, at any time and without separate notice. Do not use the platform for anything you would not want us to see.

What's not allowed

No fraud, harassment, illegal work, or arranging to take a job found here off the platform to avoid fees.

Liability

Electro Service provides the platform as is. We are not responsible for the quality of work performed by techs or for disputes between techs and providers, except as required by law.
